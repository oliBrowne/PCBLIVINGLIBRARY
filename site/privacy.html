Privacy policy

The short version: this website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. The ecadlib software runs on your computer, has no telemetry, and never sends anything to us.

This policy explains how Oliver Browne, who runs the ecadlib project ("we", "us" or "our"), handles personal information when you visit pcblivinglibrary.vercel.app (the "Site") or use the ecadlib software (the "Software"). For the Site, we are the controller of the limited data described below.

1. What the Site collects

  1. Server logs. The Site is hosted by Vercel Inc. Like any web host, Vercel's servers receive technical data when your browser requests a page: your IP address, the page requested, the time, your browser's user agent and the referring page. Vercel processes this to deliver the Site and keep it secure, and keeps it for a short period under its own policies. We do not use it to identify you and we do not combine it with other data. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  2. No cookies or tracking. The Site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, pixels, fingerprinting or advertising tools. It loads no third-party scripts or fonts.
  3. Theme setting. If you press the theme button, your choice ("light" or "dark") is saved in your browser's local storage so the Site remembers it. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it with your browser's site data settings.
  4. Contacting us. If you open a GitHub issue or message us on LinkedIn, we receive what you choose to send, along with your public profile on that platform. We use it only to reply and to improve the project. GitHub issues are public. Those platforms handle your data under their own privacy policies.

2. The Software

The Software runs on your own computer. It has no account, no telemetry and no server run by this project, so we receive nothing from it. When you use a feature that needs an outside service, the Software sends data directly from your computer to that service, using your own key where one is needed:

  • the AI provider you pick (Anthropic by default, or OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter, or a model on your own machine): datasheet pages, part numbers and pictures of generated footprints;
  • part libraries and distributors you turn on (KiCad's libraries on GitLab, DigiKey, Mouser, Nexar and others): part numbers;
  • Altium 365, GitHub or GitLab, only if you push parts or turn on the library agent.

Each of these services handles that data under its own terms. The full list, with how to turn each one off, is in PRIVACY.md. API keys you add are stored only on your computer and are never sent to us.

3. How we use and share information

We use the limited information above only to run and secure the Site and to answer you. We do not sell or rent personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models. We share it only with our hosting provider (Vercel) as needed to run the Site, or when the law requires it.

4. Legal bases (EEA and UK)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on our legitimate interests in delivering and securing the Site (for server logs) and in answering your messages (for anything you send us). Storing your theme choice on your device is strictly necessary to provide the feature you asked for.

5. International transfers

Vercel may process server logs in the United States and other countries. Where personal data from the EEA or UK is transferred, Vercel relies on safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses, as described in its own policies.

6. How long we keep it

We do not keep server logs ourselves; Vercel keeps them for a limited time under its own retention policy. Messages you send us stay on the platform you used until you or we delete them.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as those laws define it. Because we hold almost no data about you, most requests can be answered quickly. To make a request, contact us as described below. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

If you are in the EEA or UK and are unhappy with how we handled your data, you can complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

8. Children

The Site is a tool for electronics engineers and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

9. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but because the Site collects so little, there is very little to protect.

10. Changes

We may update this policy, for example if the Site ever adds analytics. The date at the top shows the latest version, and every change is public in the repository's history.

11. Contact

Open an issue on GitHub (for anything private, please say so in a short issue and we will reach out another way), or contact Oliver Browne on LinkedIn.
